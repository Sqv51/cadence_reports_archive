Job 6 Archive Manifest
======================

This archive bundle is intended for the cadence_reports_archive repository.

Included artifacts:
- job6_selective_table_summary.txt
- job6_selective_narrative_summary.txt
- job6_selective_power/ (full post-route replay reports for baseline/selective)
- job6_selective_metrics/ (metrics JSON files only, one per run)
- scripts/
  - collect_job6_selective_results.py
  - generate_benchmark_vcds_xrun.py
  - run_job6_selective_batch.sh
  - run_job6_selective_power_batch.sh

Notes:
- Large transient simulation work directories and VCD dumps are intentionally
  excluded from the archive repo.
- The archived metrics subset preserves cycle counts and patch statistics
  without carrying the full xcelium work tree.
