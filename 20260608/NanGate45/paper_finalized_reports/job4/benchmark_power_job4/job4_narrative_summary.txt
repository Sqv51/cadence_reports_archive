Job 4 Summary Notes
===================

The switched benchmark campaign was evaluated on the finalized core_hybrid_100
post-route database by replaying RTL-generated VCD activity onto the routed
design. Cycle counts were taken from the benchmark metrics JSON artifacts, and
power was extracted from Innovus report_power runs using the saved post-route DB.

Observed behavior:
- All switched binaries increased execution cycles relative to their unswitched
  baselines.
- The smallest cycle penalty was fft_butterfly at +2.705%.
- The largest cycle penalty was fir_unrolled at +51.687%.
- Core power changes were modest compared to the cycle changes.
- Four benchmarks showed lower total core power in the switched form:
  fir_direct, fir_unrolled, matmul_tiled, and outer_product.
- Two benchmarks showed slightly higher total core power in the switched form:
  complex_mul_vec and fft_butterfly.
- u_mule instance power increased consistently in switched runs, indicating the
  compiler-driven routing of multiply work onto the mule path is visible in the
  post-route power reports.

Per-benchmark deltas:
- complex_mul_vec: cycles +5.155%, core power +0.069%, u_mule 0.1204 mW
- fft_butterfly: cycles +2.705%, core power +0.126%, u_mule 0.1198 mW
- fir_direct: cycles +20.026%, core power -1.989%, u_mule 0.1343 mW
- fir_unrolled: cycles +51.687%, core power -2.551%, u_mule 0.1403 mW
- matmul_tiled: cycles +18.193%, core power -0.595%, u_mule 0.1725 mW
- outer_product: cycles +16.057%, core power -0.122%, u_mule 0.1320 mW
