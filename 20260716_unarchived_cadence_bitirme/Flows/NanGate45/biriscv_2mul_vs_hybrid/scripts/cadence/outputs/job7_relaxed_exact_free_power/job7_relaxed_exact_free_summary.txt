Job 7 Relaxed Exact-Free Oracle Sweep Summary
=============================================

Date: 2026-06-09
Target: core_hybrid_100 post-route database
Flow: relaxed static candidate filter + strict cycle-count oracle + RTL-VCD-to-post-route replay

Heuristic change
----------------

This run used the new exact-free-relaxed-mule mode. Compared with the conservative
Job 7 filter, the relaxed filter lowered the latency-distance threshold to 2 and
allowed more hot-loop/singleton candidates into the oracle trial queue.

The cycle oracle remained strict:

    accept candidate only if trial_cycles <= baseline_cycles

Result
------

The relaxed filter did not produce additional exact-free accepted patches beyond
the conservative Job 7 result. Most newly exposed candidates introduced cycle
overhead immediately and were rejected by the oracle.

Final accepted-patch table:

| `complex_bank` | 37321 | 37321 | `+0.000%` | 1 | 8.261768 | 8.267983 | `+0.075%` |

Interpretation
--------------

The relaxed policy confirmed that the strict oracle is necessary. Allowing more
candidates into the sweep did not uncover extra zero-cycle-overhead energy
reductions for this RTL implementation. The only accepted relaxed exact-free
patch preserved cycles but slightly increased total core power by 0.075%.

Therefore, this implementation does not currently demonstrate a relaxed
exact-free power benefit. The result is still useful: it shows that the proposed
oracle flow prevents the paper from accidentally claiming latency hiding for
mul-to-mule substitutions that are actually cycle-costly.
